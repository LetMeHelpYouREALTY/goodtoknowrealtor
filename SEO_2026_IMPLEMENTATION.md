# SEO 2026 Implementation Guide

## Overview
Comprehensive implementation of 2026 SEO best practices including SEO, GEO (Geographic SEO), AEO (Answer Engine Optimization), and latest Schema.org updates.

## Key Changes Implemented

### 1. Schema.org Updates for 2026
- **RealEstateListing Schema**: Replaced Product schema with RealEstateListing for property listings
- **FAQ Schema Deprecated**: Removed FAQ rich results (deprecated May 2026)
- **Person Schema**: Added comprehensive Person schema for Dr. Jan Duffy with E-E-A-T signals
- **Organization Schema**: Enhanced with 2026 requirements
- **Q&A Schema**: Replaced FAQ with QAPage for AEO optimization

### 2. AEO (Answer Engine Optimization) - 2026 Focus
**Why AEO Matters:**
- 69% of Google searches are now zero-click (up from 56% in 2024)
- ChatGPT has 883M monthly users
- Google AI Overviews appear in 55% of searches
- Traditional search volume dropping 25% by 2026

**Implementation:**
- Structured content for AI citations
- Q&A format for direct answers
- Clear, quotable statements
- Entity-rich markup
- Topical authority signals
- Fresh content timestamps

### 3. GEO (Geographic SEO) Enhancements
**Key Statistics:**
- 40% of local SEO campaigns achieve 500%+ ROI
- Organic search converts at 14.6% vs 1.7% for cold outreach

**Implementation:**
- Hyperlocal neighborhood schemas
- Expanded service area coverage
- Google Business Profile optimization markup
- Location-specific landing pages
- NAP consistency signals
- Local entity markup

### 4. E-E-A-T Signals (Experience, Expertise, Authority, Trust)
- Credentials and certifications
- Professional achievements
- Client testimonials with verification
- Industry affiliations
- Content authorship attribution
- Transparent contact information

### 5. Real Estate Specific Optimizations
- Property listing schemas with accurate pricing
- Virtual tour integration markup
- Neighborhood/community schemas
- Market statistics structured data
- Service-specific schemas
